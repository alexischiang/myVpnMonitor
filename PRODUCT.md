# Product

## Register

product

## Users

XELA operators and administrators who manage VPN subscriptions, customers, billing, payments, and 3x-ui infrastructure from a desktop or mobile admin dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Provide one operational workspace for monitoring service health, managing customer lifecycle and orders, and reviewing sales and infrastructure profitability with trustworthy accounting definitions.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, operational. The interface should feel calm under routine use and make exceptions, missing configuration, and financial risk easy to notice.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, novelty controls, dense walls of equal-priority cards, ambiguous financial labels, and color used without operational meaning.

## Design Principles

- Keep common workflows familiar and predictable.
- Separate distinct analytical jobs into focused views.
- Explain accounting and traffic definitions at the point of use.
- Prefer compact, comparable data with clear hierarchy.
- Surface incomplete data instead of implying false precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA, preserve visible keyboard focus, provide accessible names for icon-only controls, maintain 44px mobile touch targets, and avoid relying on color or hover alone.
